CMB Detection Trainer

Find cerebral microbleeds on T2*-weighted MRI, compare with the expert and with the algorithm
How to
  1. Scroll through all slices (mouse wheel, arrow keys, the slider under the picture, or one finger up and down on a phone).
  2. Click (or tap) on every cerebral microbleed you see. A ring marks it. Click the ring again to remove it.
  3. Press Score: you get precision, recall and F1 against the expert, your hits, false alarms and misses on the picture, and the result of the algorithm on the same scan.
  4. Go on with Next patient, as long as you like. Some patients have no microbleed at all: then the right answer is no marker.

What to mark: a microbleed on T2*-weighted MRI is a small, round or oval, very dark spot, mostly 2 to 10 mm across, seen on only a few slices.

Lookalikes, do not mark: vessels (dark too, but they go on over many slices or run as a line: scroll up and down before you click), calcifications (basal ganglia, choroid plexus, pineal gland), air and bone at the skull base, dark edges at the border of the brain.
